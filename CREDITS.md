# Third-party assets used in SALCI

## Illustrations

The dashboard scene banner (`src/components/shared/illustrations/SchoolSceneIllustration.tsx`) renders a Storyset illustration as the character layer.

- **"Back to school" (Rafiki style)** — `public/illustrations/back-to-school.svg`
- **"Online learning" (Cuate style)** — `public/illustrations/online-learning.svg`
- **"Teacher and student" (Rafiki style)** — `public/illustrations/teacher-student.svg`

Designed by [Storyset](https://storyset.com) (a Freepik project) under the [Freepik License](https://www.freepikcompany.com/legal#nav-freepik-agreement). Permits commercial and personal use with attribution; modification and derivative works are allowed.

Attribution is rendered here rather than inline on the banner to keep the dashboard hero scene visually clean.
